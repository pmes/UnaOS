# Screenshots
app: desktop
also: screenshot capture picture screen print screen grab

- Cmd-Shift-3 or the Print Screen key takes a picture of the whole screen.
- Cmd-Shift-4 lets you drag around a part of the screen, or click a window, to take just that. Escape cancels.

The picture is saved as a PNG file in your Desktop folder. A screenshot needs someone logged in, and the screen that shows your recovery words can never be captured.
