# Settings: Accessibility
app: settings
settings: accessibility.reduce_motion, accessibility.increase_contrast, accessibility.cursor_size, accessibility.text_size, accessibility.cvd, accessibility.invert, accessibility.zoom, accessibility.zoom_follow, accessibility.show_performance
also: accessibility vision zoom magnifier contrast motion cursor pointer large text colour blind filter invert grayscale

The Accessibility tab holds:

- Reduce motion: windows and menus appear without animation.
- Increase contrast: stronger text and lines.
- Cursor size and Text size.
- Colour filter: for red, green or blue colour blindness, or grayscale. Invert colours turns light into dark.
- Zoom: makes the screen 2, 3 or 4 times larger. Zoom follows chooses whether the view follows the pointer or the place you are typing.
- Show performance: a small panel with the computer's speed and memory, at the top right. Win-F12 shows and hides it too.

These work in every program, not just in some.
