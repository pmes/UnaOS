# The desktop
app: desktop
settings: look, dock.position, dock.autohide, display.wallpaper
also: menu bar dock taskbar start una softy look wallpaper background

The desktop is what you see after you log in. It has two looks, and you can switch between them at any time in Settings > Appearance > Look. Your windows stay open when you switch.

The Una look has a menu bar across the top and a dock of programs along the edge. The menu bar shows the menus of the program you are using, the clock, and the status of your machine. Click a dock tile to open its program. Right-click a tile for its menu.

The Softy look has a taskbar along the bottom with a Start button. Press the Windows key on its own to open or close Start.

You can move the dock, hide it until the pointer reaches it, and choose a picture for the background in Settings > General.
