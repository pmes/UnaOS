# The Launcher
app: desktop
also: search find spotlight open program app calculator sum math cmd-space

Press Cmd-Space anywhere. A field opens in the middle of the screen. Start typing.

The Launcher looks in several places at once and shows each kind in its own group: programs, files on your disk, Settings rows, help pages, shell commands, and the answer to a sum such as 2*(3+4).

Up and Down choose a result. Return opens it. A program starts, a file opens in the program that opens its type, a setting opens Settings on its row, a help page opens in the Help window, and the answer to a sum is copied so you can paste it.

Escape, or a click anywhere else, closes the Launcher. The things you pick most often move to the top of their group.
