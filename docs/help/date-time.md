# Date and time
app: settings
settings: locale.tz, locale.region, locale.first_day
also: time zone timezone clock date format 24 hour 12 hour week first day calendar

Settings > Date & Time holds:

- Time zone: where you are. The clock in the menu bar and the dates in Quarry follow it.
- Date and time format: the order of day, month and year, and a 24-hour or 12-hour clock.
- First day of week.

A change shows at once. In the shell window, "date" shows the clock.
