# Keyboard layouts
app: settings
settings: keyboard.layout, keyboard.compose
also: keyboard layout language german french spanish italian swedish swiss british uk us qwerty azerty qwertz accent dead keys compose altgr

Settings > Keyboard holds your keyboard layout: U.S., British, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Swedish and Finnish, or Swiss German. Each layout carries its accent keys and its AltGr characters.

Compose key: when it is on, a tap of the right Alt key on its own starts a composed character. The next two keys make it: s then s makes the German sharp s, = then e makes the euro sign.

Your choice is saved in Settings. Typing through the layout you choose is still being joined up with every keyboard, so some keys may still type as on a U.S. keyboard.
