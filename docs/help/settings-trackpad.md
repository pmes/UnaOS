# Settings: Trackpad
app: settings
settings: trackpad.speed, trackpad.tap_to_click, trackpad.natural_scroll, trackpad.secondary_click, trackpad.three_finger_drag
also: trackpad touchpad tap click scroll natural two finger right click drag

The Trackpad tab holds the tracking speed and four switches: Tap to click, Natural scrolling, Secondary click with two fingers, and Three-finger drag. Each change applies as soon as you make it.
