# Settings: Appearance
app: settings
settings: appearance.mode, appearance.accent, appearance.highlight, look
also: dark mode light mode colour color accent highlight theme look una softy

The Appearance tab holds:

- Appearance: Light, Dark, or Auto, which follows the time of day.
- Accent colour: the colour of buttons, selections and the focus ring.
- Highlight colour: the colour behind selected text.
- Look: Una, with the menu bar and the dock, or Softy, with the taskbar and Start. The change happens at once and your windows stay open.
