# Windows
app: desktop
also: window move close minimize snap switch zoom title bar focus

Every program shows itself in a window.

- Move a window by dragging its title bar.
- Double-click the title bar to make the window large, and again to put it back.
- Close a window with the close box at the corner of its title bar, or Cmd-W.
- Cmd-M minimizes the window. Cmd-H hides the program.
- Cmd-Tab, or Alt-Tab, moves to the next program. Cmd-` moves to the next window of the same program.
- Cmd-Alt-Left and Cmd-Alt-Right put a window on the left or right half of the screen.
- Cmd-Q quits the program. Cmd-Alt-Esc opens Force Quit for a program that does not answer.

A window that opens while you are typing somewhere else does not take your keyboard. It opens behind your window and its dock tile pulses once, so you can go to it when you are ready.
