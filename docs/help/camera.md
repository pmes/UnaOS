# The camera
also: camera webcam video live view photo shutter sony ptp uvc usb

A USB camera that sends video is shown in a window named Camera, which opens on the first picture.

The first time, you are asked whether Camera may use the camera: Not now, or Allow. Allow is kept; you can take it back in Settings > Privacy.

While any camera is on, a camera mark with a dot shows in the menu bar. No program can turn the mark off while the camera is in use.

A camera that speaks PTP, as many photo cameras do in their PC mode, is reached from the shell window: "ptp" shows its state, "ptp view" opens the live view in the Camera window (Space takes a picture), and "ptp shutter" takes a picture.
