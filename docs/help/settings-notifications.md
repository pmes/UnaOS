# Settings: Notifications
app: settings
settings: notify.dnd, notify.dnd_from
also: notifications notices toasts do not disturb quiet hours schedule

Notices appear at the corner of the screen, at most three at a time, for five seconds each. Hold the pointer over one to keep it. A notice that does not fit waits its turn; none is thrown away to make room.

Do Not Disturb keeps notices off the screen. Scheduled turns it on by itself between two times of day that you choose.
