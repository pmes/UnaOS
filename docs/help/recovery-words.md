# Recovery words
also: recovery words key forgot password lost encryption unlock disk locked 24 words

When the first user is made on a computer whose disk is locked with your password, the login screen shows 24 recovery words once. Write them on paper and keep the paper somewhere safe.

The words are shown only that one time. They are never saved on the computer and never sent anywhere, and the screen that shows them can not be captured.

If you forget your password, type the 24 words in the unlock field when the computer starts. They open the disk just as your password does.

Nobody else, not the makers of this system either, can open your disk without your password or your words.
