# Phones and cameras as disks
also: phone android mtp ptp usb transfer photos pictures card memory card stick

Plug in a phone set to File transfer and it appears in Quarry as a folder under Volumes, named after the phone, with its storage inside, such as its internal storage and its card. You can open and copy its files to your computer.

Copying files onto the phone is not available yet.

A memory card or a USB stick appears in Quarry the same way.
