## The five gaps that would most change what the board can DO — ranked

1. **One core, no tick (SMP + periodic EL1 timer + preemption).** Six cores are online and proven
   (PSCI CPU_ON, SGIs, per-AP ticks, and under `rastmc` they rendered), yet the shipped image does
   everything on core 0 cooperatively with no timer after the drop; the APs idle at EL2 and the
   scheduler's EL1 filter makes them unplaceable, so the render pass, the console pump, USB
   servicing and any EL0 tenant all share one poll-spinning core, and `bg` is refused outright.
   The pieces exist unshipped (`bsptick`, `bsprun`, `orinel1ap`), and the EL1 IRQ path is already
   proven by the one-shot. **First metal question:** with `UNAOS_BSPTICK=1`, does the boot core's
   CNTP PPI 30 keep re-arming at EL1 across the JM6 latch (`HCR_EL2 IMO=0/FMO=0`,
   `CNTHCTL_EL2=0x3`) — i.e. does `[bsptick]` count past 1 while the JD2 pump still progresses
   without its `hlt()` poll-spin? (Lane: jetson for `timer.rs`/`boot_tegra.rs`; `sched.rs`
   changes need the rmbp grant; Peter's SMP D1–D5 ruling is still pending per the resume.)

2. **Network RX (NET-4A).** The RTL8168 is found, linked, MAC'd, bound to smoltcp, and its
   DISCOVER leaves the wire — but RX completions latch a single buffer (17) and no OFFER is ever
   delivered; the Orin has never held a lease, answered an ARP, or been pinged. Everything downstream
   (NTP for the RTC row, remote logging, a network install path) waits on this. **First metal
   question:** with the RX ring and its 32 buffers placed *below 4 GiB* (no inbound-iATU alias,
   which the 32-bit `addr` field forced), do four consecutive RX completions land in four distinct
   buffers (`[net4F] buffers-written(count=4)`)? If yes, NET-4A was the alias; if no, it is the
   NIC/RC and the next question is the RC's inbound iATU region ordering. (Lane: jetson.)

3. **A block device that is not a USB stick (on-die SDMMC write, then NVMe).** The only storage
   today is BOT over a USB3 card reader; the Tegra SDHCI reaches card identification and sector-0
   reads read-only under a knob, and `program_source` refuses `TegraSd` by construction. Self-update,
   the flight recorder, an unafs root and "future sessions can write the files and warm reboot"
   (Peter's mission ruling) all need a writable non-removable volume. NVMe is the fuller answer
   but is L (no PCIe driver for C4, no NVMe driver, anywhere). **First metal question:** on
   `mmc@3400000` with the vendor pad block left DISABLED (the SError conviction), does a CMD24
   single-block write to a scratch sector followed by CMD17 read-back return the written bytes,
   at 1-bit default speed? (Lane: jetson for `sdmmc_tegra.rs`; `block.rs`/`fat.rs` need the rmbp
   grant to return `TegraSd` as a program source.)
   **In tree (orin 14, A23):** the `sdmmcwrite` probe asks exactly this — `UNAOS_SDMMC=1 UNAOS_SDMMCWRITE=1`,
   one CMD24 to the top of the post-MBR gap, printed before the write, CMD17 read-back over all 512 bytes;
   `docs/dev/evidence/orin14/SDMMCWRITE.md` carries the wire and the scorer. Unflown.

4. **Serial RX (ORINRX).** TX is the whole instrument; RX is guarded to `None` and the render2
   injection delivered nothing. Without it every flight is attended-only: no remote shell, no
   scripted bench, no "type at the board" from the seat — the operational multiplier for every
   other row. It is S-sized. **First metal question:** after DARKWIN-GUARD `ready()`, with one
   byte injected over the butler FIFO, does a raw read of `LSR` at 0x0C280000+5 show `DR=1`
   (and `RBR` the byte) — or is the FIFO being drained by the SPE/TCU first, which would move
   the fix to a different UART (the `serial.rs:45` fallback bases)? (Lane: jetson.)
   **Answered on render3b (2026-09-05, A4):** LSR raw reads `0x200` → RX-LIVE, and 3 of 5 injected
   bytes reached the shell behind `orinrx`; the open question moved to A16 (which reader drops the
   other two, and whether an FCR write would help or harm the SPE co-owner).

5. **USB interrupts + post-boot hot-plug.** The xHCI is polled with `IMAN.IE` set and zero
   interrupts taken, and after the EL2→EL1 drop only `poll_events` runs — hubs, storage and new
   devices attached after the 60 s window are never serviced, and the pump's `hlt()` waits are
   why (they assume no timer on the EL1 core). This is the first *device* SPI the Orin would ever
   route and it depends on gap 1. **First metal question:** with the DTB `interrupts` cell of
   `usb@3610000` enabled at the GICD and unmasked on the EL1 core, does one port-status-change
   event raise `IRQ_COUNT` above 0 without an EL3 abort or a stuck `EHB`? (Lane: jetson for the
   GIC arm; `drivers/xhci/` is rmbp-owned.)

Not in the five, stated so they are not read as forgotten: **GPU** (was RULED OUT by §4 and the
signed-microcode fact; Peter's R18 2026-09-06 reopened it as a boot-by-boot ladder — `GA10B-LADDER.md`, rung 2 fixed-unflown, §F row); **display engine / vblank** (RULED OUT for
the ladder; EL3-fatal aperture); **WiFi** (L; card identity not even measured on PCIe); **audio /
GPIO / I2C / SPI / RTC** (each S–M and each behind the proven BPMP channel, but none changes what
the board does before 1–5 do); **watchdog / SYSTEM_RESET / prtscr / heap size** (each S, each a
one-knob or one-constant change worth taking on the next flight rather than an arc).
