# BP-gpu — the GPU drivers under drivers/gpu: the gen7 (Ivy Bridge) ladder (read at 8c2767110)

## 1. What it is
- `drivers::gpu` is x86_64-only (`unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/mod.rs:33-34`); `unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/gpu/mod.rs` declares the Kepler ladder (`kepler`, `kepler_display`, `kepler_fifo`, `kepler_ce`, `kepler_gpublit`, `kepler_vblank`, `pcihealth`; R101, R127), the IGD init `igpu` (BAR0 window, its own BLT ring), `detect`, and `gen7` (`unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/gpu/mod.rs:77-78`). This sheet binds the gen7 family; the Kepler rungs are named here and owed their rows (§5 GPU-SHEET-KEPLER).
- gen7 is the Intel HD 4000 GT2 of the 2012 rMBP: `unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/gpu/gen7.rs` (R1 recon, R2 wake, R3 forcewake, R4 GGTT claim, R5 execute and R6 rearm on the RCS, R7 blit, R8 `fb_blit` and R8b band on the BCS, `mod ladder`, the hold rule at its tail), its children `gen7_blit.rs` (the BCS as the compositor's second blitter, `bcs_job`), `gen7_r9.rs` (R9 pipeline select / state base / PIPE_CONTROL) > `gen7_r10.rs` (R10 3DPRIMITIVE) > `gen7_r11.rs` (R11 textured quad), all under `unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/gpu/`. Two engines: RCS (render) and BCS (blitter).
- The panel is the Kepler's: no gen7 rung scans out. Design and ladder history: `docs/dev/OS/08_VIDEO/gen7.md`.
- aarch64 has no `drivers/gpu`: the Pi's V3D (`unaos/crates/kernel/src/arch/aarch64/v3d.rs`), the Orin's GA10B probe rungs (`unaos/crates/kernel/src/arch/aarch64/ga10b_probe.rs` and its siblings) and the Tegra display (`unaos/crates/kernel/src/arch/aarch64/display_tegra.rs`) live in the arch tree (§5 GPU-SHEET-ARM).

## 3. Seams
- The boot reaches gen7 through ONE call: `igpu::init` -> `gen7::bank`. The run reaches it through ONE fixture: `tests.rs` `ensure_gen7` -> `gen7::ladder_test` (ladder, `blit::test`, `hold_leg`).
- The compositor (`video/blitter.rs`, KCOMP's trait) reaches the BCS only through `selected()` / `arm_bcs` / `demote_bcs`; gen7 never calls wm or a painter.
- The heap's books: gen7 reaches them only by `allocator::census::retag(.., GEN7_HELD)` on a fresh held block (BP-cores-22; the once-count is video.md BP-video-18).
- igpu's BLT ring and the gen7 BCS are never both live (BP-gpu-3).

## 5. Ruled, unbuilt / owed
- GEN7-HELD-RELEASE (GEN7HOLDONCE B1025's; GEN7HELDRELEASE rmbp-ledger B1039, in flight): a held page goes back only after the ring reads back disabled AND a WITNESSED GGTT TLB flush, one `[gen7] held release` line per release, refused by name where the flush cannot be witnessed; it CHANGES BP-gpu-8 and BP-gpu-7's "never the sole reason" stands.
- GEN7-HELD-WIRE (GEN7HELDONE B1031's; B1039 carries it): r11's own `[gen7] r11` line has no `reuse= held_kb=` term.
- GEN7-HELD-PARK: BUILT by GEN7HELDPARK (rmbp-ledger B1067): measured, B1039's one release site already runs on the park's path: the compositor's one engine park (the stall demote in `BcsBlitter::blit`, gen7_blit.rs) is reached only after `bcs_job`'s tail `held_release` refused `ring-not-disabled` (the ring did not drain, its PTEs stay claimed), so no second release site is built; `bcs_job` never parks (bounded `poll_cycles`). The park's keep is now said: `held_release`'s once-bit is keyed by engine and class (`kept_bit`, gen7.rs tail), so an earlier `flush-write-silent` keep no longer silences it; GEN7ORDER's `park_kat=` is its pure KAT. GEN7-PARK-UNWIND: BUILT by GEN7PARKUNWIND (rmbp-ledger B1073): measured, the demote is final (`demote_bcs` clears the selection, `arm_bcs` is only `blit::test`'s, which replays), so no later frame reaches gen7 and `tests gen7`'s `ladder_test` is the one re-check point; a stall (armed, not drained) parks its window in `PAGES`' own payload (no new lock or slot) and keeps the ring's START/HEAD/TAIL; `park_unwind` (gen7_blit.rs tail) reads RING_CTL and HEAD under the job's forcewake candidate, judges them by `park_quiesced` (R7's `r7_engine_quiesced`, gen7.rs tail; its KAT on `park_kat=`) and on a quiesce runs bcs_job's own `ring_restore` and `window_unwind` (the PTE restore, the flush, `held_release`). GEN7-PARK-NODEMOTE: BUILT by GEN7PARKNODEMOTE (rmbp-ledger B1077): measured, `bcs_job` already parked an armed, drained ring whose RING_CTL would not clear (`out.armed && !all_disabled`) but `BcsBlitter::blit`'s stall arm keyed on `!idle` alone, so it said `Unavailable` and every later frame paid an engine take and BAR0 reads for a `window-owned` refusal; the stall arm now also takes `!disabled` (JobOut's RING_CTL read-back), so the one demote runs once, and the keep says `held_kept=blit:ring-ctl-stuck` once (`held_release`'s body lifted as `held_release_kept`, gen7.rs; `kept_bit`'s third class, its bit checked distinct on `park_kat=`); `park_quiesced` refuses a live CTL, so the park unwinds only through GEN7-PARK-FROZEN. Owed: GEN7-PARK-FROZEN (a HEAD frozen short of TAIL at the disable, or a RING_CTL that never clears, never reads quiesced: kept for the boot; a BCS idle register needs a PRM pin), GEN7-PARK-FRAME (a per-frame re-check needs a compositor hook), GEN7-PARK-UNARMED-LIVE (a candidate that never read armed but whose RING_CTL will not clear leaves `armed` false: `Unavailable`, then `bcs-ring-live` per job, no demote, no park), GEN7-PARK-WORD (the stall's keep, CTL cleared and HEAD short, still says `ring-not-disabled`; `ring-not-drained` would rename B1067's class), GEN7-PARK-SPANS (a parked window's span PTEs map the caller's pages, which gen7 does not hold), GEN7-PARK-R8: BUILT by GEN7PARKR8 (rmbp-ledger B1078): measured, an unarmed first R8 (DONE unset) re-enters `fb_blit` on `hold_leg`'s second ladder run, after a `blit::test` that can park and before `park_unwind`, and R8 never took the engine; `r8_engine_clear` (gen7.rs tail) reads the park through the same `blit::engine_take` door `park_unwind` uses and drops it before the arm: a held park or a live job refuses on the rung's own verdict line (`r8-skip(parked|busy)`), DONE stays unset so the next `tests gen7` arms R8; owed GEN7-R8-DOOR (a compositor job that starts between that read and R8's arm is not excluded).
- GPU-SHEET-ARM: the aarch64 GPU paths (V3D, GA10B, Tegra display, §1) are under no sheet: their own rows or sheet.
- GPU-SHEET-KEPLER: the Kepler ladder's invariants (takeover, CE channel, fifo, vblank wait, pcihealth) carry no BP ids yet.
