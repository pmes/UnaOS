# BP-audio — sinks, sources, HDA, USB Audio Class 1, Bluetooth A2DP (read at 96ed4711 + UAC1, rmbp-ledger B706; + UACMIC B723; + A2DP B739)

## 1. What it is
- ONE seam: `unaos/crates/kernel/src/audio/mod.rs` (cfg `uvc` or `hda-tone`). `audio::sink(name)` chooses where sound goes, `audio::tone_on(sink, hz, ms)` plays to it; the choice is Principia's `system.audio.output` (`hda` | `usb`).
- HDA (x86, `hda` + `hda-tone`): the Intel HDA controller walk and the output stream (`unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/hda.rs`, `hda_stream.rs`, `hda_play.rs`, `hda_amp.rs`); the tone is a test (R77), `tests hda`.
- USB Audio Class 1 playback and capture (cfg `uac`, its own feature since UACMIC B723; it implies `uvc`, whose engine it rides; `UNAOS_UAC=1` arms it and attaches QEMU's `usb-audio`): `unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/usb/uac.rs` over the host-neutral `uvc::IsoHost`, carried by xHCI isochronous OUT in `unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/xhci/isoch.rs` (the XHCIISO engine, B534, now both directions).
- ONE capture seam (UACMIC B723): `audio::source(app)` → a `Source` (`read`, `format`, closed on drop) over the class driver's 64 KiB capture ring; the kernel's recorder names `audio::KERNEL_RECORDER`, any other app needs Principia's `system.decisions.microphone.<app>` (`fs::apptrust::MIC_DOMAIN`) or gets ONE ask. The menu bar's mic dot (`video::menubar::micdot`) paints from the driver's count.
- Settings > General, the Sound box's Mute row: the output picker `HDA internal | USB <vid:pid>` (`unaos/crates/kernel/src/video/settings.rs:952` paint, `:1718` press).
- The decoders (WAV/FLAC/Ogg/MP3/AAC) are `audio_core` (shared, no_std); the kernel Player is a mixer source (`drivers/hda_play.rs`, BP-audio-15).
- THE MIXER (AUDIOMIX, rmbp-ledger B731): `unaos/crates/kernel/src/audio/mixer.rs` — N sources (a ring-3 program's `SYS_AUDIO_*` stream, a kernel source), each PCM16/2/48k with its own gain, mixed per 10 ms period (saturating add, master volume and mute) into the current sink; `tests audiomix`.
- USB Audio Class 1 playback (cfg `uvc`): `unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/usb/uac.rs` over the host-neutral `uvc::IsoHost`, carried by xHCI isochronous OUT in `unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/xhci/isoch.rs` (the XHCIISO engine, B534, now both directions).
- Settings > General, the Sound box's Mute row: the output picker `HDA internal | USB <vid:pid>` (`unaos/crates/kernel/src/video/settings.rs:984` paint, `:1753` press).
- The Player, the video's sound, the alert (BP-audio-15) and Stria (ring 3) are mixer sources; the mixer is the only writer of the chosen sink.
- Bluetooth A2DP source (A2DP B739, cfg `a2dp` since A2DPKNOB B750 — it implies `btc`; x86): a paired headset is a third sink — `audio::sink("bt")`, fed through `unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/bt/avdtp.rs`'s PCM ring, framed into SBC by `sbc_core` (`unaos/libs/media/sbc_core`, shared, no_std; the kernel includes its `src/sbc.rs` by path) and sent as AVDTP media packets over the BTHID host's L2CAP (net.md BP-net-14).

## 3. Seams
- `audio` may call: `drivers::hda` (one call), `drivers::usb::uac` (begin/write/finish/queued/counters), `prefs` (the one key).
- `uac` may call: `uvc::IsoHost` (its host), `audio::usb_arrived` / `usb_gone`. `isoch` calls (through its `ac` door) `uac::pull` / `out_td_len` / `cfg_has_audio` / `probe`, and for capture `push_capture` / `capture_due` / `capture_service` / `capture_lost`.
- `audio::source` may call `uac::capture_open` / `read_capture` / `capture_close` and `prefs::decided` / `decide` (the microphone domain); the menu bar reads `uac::capture_armed` only.
- `uac` may call: `uvc::IsoHost` (its host), `audio::usb_arrived` / `usb_gone`. `isoch` calls `uac::pull` / `out_td_len` / `cfg_has_audio` / `probe` / `cfg_has_hid` / `disconnect`.
- MICRING3 (B736): the x86 bus dispatch calls `audio::bus_source` only; `fs::apptrust::mic_revoked` calls `audio::source_revoked`; `sched::term_release_edge` calls `audio::slot_gone`. The ring-3 table reaches the driver through the same `capture_open` / `read_capture` / `capture_close`.
- Settings calls `audio::picker` and `audio::sink` only.

## 5. Ruled, unbuilt / open
- Feedback (asynchronous) endpoints: a 48-frame-per-ms stream is sent unmetered; an async device's clock drift is unhandled. UAC2 (high-speed, clock entities): refused.
- The Player, Stria and the alert sound still write the HDA stream directly; moving them onto `audio::sink` is owed. Volume/mute reach HDA only (no feature-unit SET_CUR on USB).
- The class is the `uac` feature (UACMIC B723); its declaration line in `drivers/mod.rs` still names `uvc` (the file erases itself with `#![cfg(feature = "uac")]`; moving the word is owed).
- QEMU: `UNAOS_UAC=1` (alias `UNAOS_USBAUDIO=1`) attaches `usb-audio` (`unaos/arroyo`); the rmbp bench flights with a USB speaker and a USB microphone are unflown.
- A2DP (B739): the mixer (`audio/mixer.rs`, AUDIOMIX B731) outputs to USB or HDA only — the Bluetooth sink takes the tone path (`audio::bt_tone`) only; moving it under the mixer is owed. SBC only (no AAC/aptX); no delay reporting sent; outbound L2CAP fragmentation built (A2DPKNOB B750; a media packet is bounded by two ACL packets); the SBC KAT is not byte-exact against a published frame (owed); one A2DP device at a time; the stream's clock is the kernel's ms clock (no feedback from the sink). QEMU has no Bluetooth: the rmbp bench flight with a headset is unflown.
- Capture: ring 3 reaches `audio::source` through bus verbs 40..42 (MICRING3 B736, `uac` only, x86 only); Settings > Privacy's Revoke calls `fs::apptrust::mic_revoked` beside the camera's `uvc::revoked` (`privacy_service`), closing an open source; the Softy look's mic light is SOFTYMIC's (B749, `softy/taskbar.rs`).
