# BP-audio — sinks, sources, HDA, USB Audio Class 1, Bluetooth A2DP (read at 96ed4711 + UAC1, rmbp-ledger B706; + UACMIC B723; + A2DP B739; de-duplicated by AUDIOCAPTURE B851)

## 1. What it is
- ONE seam: `unaos/crates/kernel/src/audio/mod.rs` (cfg `uvc` or `hda-tone`). `audio::sink(name)` chooses where sound goes, `audio::tone_on(sink, hz, ms)` plays to it; the choice is Principia's `system.audio.output` (`hda` | `usb`).
- HDA (x86, `hda` + `hda-tone`): the Intel HDA controller walk and the output stream (`unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/hda.rs`, `hda_stream.rs`, `hda_play.rs`, `hda_amp.rs`); the tone is a test (R77), `tests hda`.
- THE HDA RINGS (HDARING, rmbp-ledger B811): the controller's ONE CORB/RIRB pair is the DEVICE's — `hda::ring` (`unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/hda.rs` file tail: `own` / `attach` / `hold` / `bring_up`); `probe`, `vol::apply`, `vol_readback` and `amp::svc_rings` all take it there.
- USB Audio Class 1 playback and capture (cfg `uac`, its own feature since UACMIC B723; it implies `uvc`, whose engine it rides; `UNAOS_UAC=1` arms it and attaches QEMU's `usb-audio`): `unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/usb/uac.rs` over the host-neutral `uvc::IsoHost`, carried by xHCI isochronous OUT in `unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/xhci/isoch.rs` (the XHCIISO engine, B534, now both directions).
- ONE capture seam (UACMIC B723): `audio::source(app)` → a `Source` (`read`, `format`, closed on drop) over the class driver's 64 KiB capture ring; the kernel's recorder names `audio::KERNEL_RECORDER`, any other app needs Principia's `system.decisions.microphone.<app>` (`fs::apptrust::MIC_DOMAIN`) or gets ONE ask. The menu bar's mic dot (`video::menubar::micdot`) paints from the driver's count.
- Settings > General, the Sound box's Mute row: the output picker `HDA internal | USB <vid:pid>` (`unaos/crates/kernel/src/video/settings.rs:984` paint, `:1753` press).
- The decoders (WAV/FLAC/Ogg/MP3/AAC) are `audio_core` (shared, no_std); the kernel Player is a mixer source (`drivers/hda_play.rs`, BP-audio-17). Vorbis takes the Ogg granule trims of Vorbis I §A.2 (`unaos/libs/media/audio_core/src/vorbis/mod.rs` `OggVorbis::handle`: the first audio packet yields nothing, a first-page granule below the decoded count trims the start, the EOS page's granule trims the end, a page both first and last cuts the end; a start-trimmed stream was also end-cut by the trim amount, fixed by VORBISTRIM LEDGER SR307, exercised by seek_kat `vorbis_granule_trims` and lossy_oracle, whose one Chromium divergence is FFmpeg's first-packet phantom on a first-and-EOS page). Owed: chained Ogg streams (VORBISTRIM-CHAIN: `unaos/libs/media/audio_core/src/ogg.rs` ends decoding at the chain boundary).
- THE MIXER (AUDIOMIX, rmbp-ledger B731): `unaos/crates/kernel/src/audio/mixer.rs` — N sources (a ring-3 program's `SYS_AUDIO_*` stream, a kernel source), each PCM16/2/48k with its own gain, mixed per 10 ms period (saturating add, master volume and mute) into the current sink; `tests audiomix`.
- The Player, the video's sound, the alert (BP-audio-17) and Stria (ring 3) are mixer sources; the mixer is the only writer of the chosen sink.
- Bluetooth A2DP source (A2DP B739, cfg `a2dp` since A2DPKNOB B750 — it implies `btc`; x86): a paired headset is a third sink — `audio::sink("bt")`, fed through `unaos/crates/kernel/src/drivers/bt/avdtp.rs`'s PCM ring, framed into SBC by `sbc_core` (`unaos/libs/media/sbc_core`, shared, no_std; the kernel includes its `src/sbc.rs` by path) and sent as AVDTP media packets over the BTHID host's L2CAP (net.md BP-net-14).
