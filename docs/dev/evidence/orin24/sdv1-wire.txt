Source: the Orin metal serial capture of render12 boot 2, 2026-09-09, with a 1.9 GB FAT card
(label UNAOS-DATA, plain FAT, no UnaOS content) in the Orin's microSD slot. Extracted verbatim by
`awk 'index($0,"SDMMC")'` plus the `[vfs] root =` line; nothing else is changed, elided or reordered.

The defect is the CMD55 line: CMD8 going unanswered is the CORRECT answer from an SD v1.x (SDSC)
card, and the ladder says so and continues — but the very next command times out too, and the run
ends there with `tegra-sd=absent`. Analysis and fix: docs/dev/OS/01_BOOT_HAL/arch_arm64.md,
section ORIN-SDMMC-1 / SDV1.
