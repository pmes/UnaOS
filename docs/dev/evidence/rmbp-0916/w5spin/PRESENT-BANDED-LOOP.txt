WHAT IT IS
  A test-and-test-and-set acquire of `PACE_SHADOW[slot]`'s spin byte — `pace_shadow_refresh`'s
  `let mut buf = PACE_SHADOW[slot].lock();` (unaos/crates/kernel/src/video/wm.rs:2119), inlined
  into `present_banded`. `PACE_SHADOW` is the WPACE-TEXT per-window present-boundary shadow
  (wm.rs:2042), 32 bytes per `Mutex<Vec<u8>>` entry — hence the `shlq $0x5`.

  There is no exit from f9da0..f9da8 except the byte going to zero. No budget, no counter, no
  deadline, no yield. The three dead cores of flight 10 (c1 at 665828 ms, c2 at 668402 ms, c3 at
  841827 ms) were all inside these four instructions, at two rips four bytes apart, with
  `taken=y` (they answered the NMI, so they are not architecturally parked) and `in_blit=n`
  (rip outside memcpy, and `blit_inflight=0` on two of the three tripwires).
