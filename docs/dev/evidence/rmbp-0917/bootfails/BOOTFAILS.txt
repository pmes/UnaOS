2. THE CAUSE, AND IT IS NOT THE PANEL
   Both reds need one precondition: the MENU BAR IS ENABLED. `desktop_uefi::activate` enables it
   (`[wc-x] menubar ENABLED panel=2880x1800 rect=Some((0, 0, 2880, 34))`, capture lines 1525 / 8226 /
   13229) and `activate` has exactly one caller, the Kepler takeover — so on the QEMU x86 lane the bar
   is OFF at both fixtures (`[wm] tile-fit ... work=0+724`) and `move_vacate_probe` never runs at all.
   `UNAOS_FBW`/`UNAOS_FBH` do NOT reach this: they are read by `arch/aarch64/mailbox.rs` only
   (`grep -rn 'UNAOS_FBW' --include=*.rs` = 1 declaration site, aarch64), so the x86 gate cannot be
   driven to 2880x1800 with them and a 2880x1800 x86 lane does not exist.

   move-vacate: the probe pins its box at `(EDGE_GAP, EDGE_GAP)` = (8,8). `wm::create_at` clamps a
   pinned CONTENT origin to `work_top + TITLE_H + BORDER` = 34 + 34 + 5 = 73, and the capture's own
   `[wc-d] verify win=2 ... at (13,73) panel=2880x1800` (line 13248) is the clamp firing. The probe
   then reads back at the coordinates it ASKED for (content origin (13,47)), which the window never
   occupied: `painted=false`, and the title-strip sample `(ax+ow/2, ay+TITLE_H/2)` = (85,25) lands in
   the bar — neither DESKTOP_BG nor PROBE_COL — which is the 4/5.

   clickroute leg 5: `desktop_pt` is the first of four PANEL corners with `wm::hit_test(..).is_none()`.
   hit_test answers for WINDOW ROWS only; the bar and the dock are FURNITURE and invisible to it, so
   (2,2) reads "unowned" and `wc_click_route_at` routes it to the menu band:
   `14335  [clickroute] press at (2,2) band=menu -> open deliver=0`. Consumed, but the keyboard is
   never handed to the shell, so `desktop=false`. On this panel ALL FOUR corners are furniture
   (work area = `work=34+1604`, so rows 0..33 are bar and 1638..1799 are dock).
   SECOND-ORDER, and it is why `deflect=true` is not a verdict on any of the three boots: the menu leg
   5 opened stays open, so leg 7's furniture press is eaten by the band as a `dismiss`
   (`14339  [clickroute] press at (1354,491) band=menu -> dismiss`) and NEVER reaches the furniture
   arm — no `[clickroute] furniture deflect` line exists in any of the three metal boots, while the
   QEMU lane prints one.
