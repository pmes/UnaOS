## 3. Findings
- **REDBAND (new, boot 1, the glass)**: the PUMPWEDGE band (`sessionwedge.rs` `paint`, straight onto the framebuffer at `height − 3·band_h`, theme WEDGE_BAND 0x601010) is painted while the usb-pump is wedged and "cleared" by `paint("")` — which FILLS the same rectangle with the band colour and never damages the compositor's scene. After the 2.2 s dongle wedge the band stayed; the cursor restore and the app bar overpainted it piecemeal ("mouse takes a bite of it. app bar wrote over it"). Fix: on clear, damage the rect (a compositor repaint), not a fill. Small; the seat did not touch it (R76: it boots).
- **SPLASHWORD (new, boot 2, the glass)**: "Resolving the session" stayed painted under the login window — the splash's stage word is not erased when the login screen comes up on the boot-2 path (the same direct-framebuffer class as REDBAND).
- **POINTERFEEL (open; the fixtures do not measure it)**: Peter: "mouse completely fucking terrible in nearly every way  can only just get it to work". On the wire: trackpad `route=legacy` (the vendor multi-touch route not taken; `ids=02:229,44:0`); `[login] … hid_gap_max_ms=1028` (a one-second hole in the HID stream at the login screen, bound 50); `[ptr] fine n=219` unchanged for 4 s while `[tp] mt … dx=0 dy=-1` says a finger was down (reports stopped arriving); `[hid] stall census holder=usb-pump:flight-recorder worst_ms=234 → HELD`, `[lag] stall stage=hid 94 ms`. Yet typed on boot 2: HIDSTALL PASS, FINEMOTION 200/200 PASS, INPUTSTALL PASS (`events=0`). FINEMOTION injects deltas; INPUTSTALL saw no events; the fixtures measure the kernel's own path, not the trackpad's report stream under a finger. Owed: a fixture that counts REAL reports per second while a finger is down and names every gap > 16 ms with its holder; the multi-touch route; the flight-recorder hold off the pump.
