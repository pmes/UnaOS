After the gmux routes the panel to the IGD, nothing automatically brings the iGPU pipe or plane up. As established in the pull 7 canon, the iGPU reads "all-dead" pre- and post-ExitBootServices (e.g., `PP_STATUS`, `DSPACNTR`, and `DP_A` are zeroed or inactive). The firmware did not leave the display pipeline configured. Therefore, the answer is: **we must program it**. A successful mux switch alone only moves the electrical routing; to achieve a non-zero liveness criterion (like a ticking `PIPE_FRMCOUNT_A` or an active `DSPACNTR`), Flight 1 must explicitly program the iGPU display pipeline (PLLs, panel power sequencer, link training, pipe timings, and plane configuration) to drive the panel.
