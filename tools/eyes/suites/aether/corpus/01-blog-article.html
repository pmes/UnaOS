On Building a Browser from Scratch

Posted October 4 by the Aether team

Every browser starts with a parser. The parser turns bytes into a tree, and the tree is the thing every later stage reads. Styles attach to it, layout measures it, and paint walks it.

Three stages

  1. Parse the document into a DOM.
  2. Resolve styles through the cascade.
  3. Lay out boxes and paint them.
A page that looks wrong should arrive with a list of why.

What we skip

  • Hardware compositing
  • Process isolation

The entry point is render_frame(), which paints the damage rects:

fn main() {
    let engine = Engine::new();
    engine.render_frame();
}
