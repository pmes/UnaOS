Pointer

  • Move over a link: the pointer becomes a hand and the status line names the link.
  • Click a link to open it. Drag over text to select it.
  • The wheel scrolls; the middle button drags the page.

Keys

  • Tab focuses the next link, Enter follows it.
  • Backspace or Left goes back, Right forward.
  • Ctrl+C copies the selection to the clipboard.
  • Esc clears the selection, then closes the window.

Find

Ctrl+F or / opens the find bar. Type to search the page, Enter steps to the next match, Esc closes the bar.
